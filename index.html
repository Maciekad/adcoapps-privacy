Privacy Policy

Multitrack Memos

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Multitrack Memos is a simple voice recorder with multitrack recording and basic mixing features, designed for songwriters.

Data Storage

Recordings, audio tracks, and notes are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your recordings.

Microphone Access

Microphone access is used for audio recording.

Data Collection

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. You can choose to export or share recordings using your device's sharing features.
